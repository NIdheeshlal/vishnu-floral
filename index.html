About

Florist · Floral designer · Fruit arrangement artist

Flowers above, hand-carved fruit below — built as one piece.

Five years designing for UAE retail and events. I make custom bouquets, vase arrangements, event displays and large signature centrepieces that sit on a bed of fresh fruit with a watermelon carved by hand — a swan, a dove, a koi pair, a horse.

My work has run through high-traffic retail, including the floral counter at Carrefour Yas Mall in Abu Dhabi, and now at Ambrosia, Fresh Market in Dubai. I match flower type, colour and size to the occasion and the budget.

I also hold a Diploma in Air-Conditioning and Refrigeration, which matters more in this trade than it sounds: I understand chiller and cold-room conditions, so stock stays fresh longer and less is wasted.

The Signature

Carved by hand

Under the flowers on every signature centrepiece sits a bed of fresh fruit, and at its centre a watermelon carved by hand into a swan, a dove, a koi pair, a horse, a phoenix or a bloom of roses.

It is cut fresh for each piece, it cannot be reused, and it is the part people photograph. Ten of the twenty-six designs here carry one.

About

Florist with five years of UAE retail experience, designing for walk-in, phone and event customers.

I design custom bouquets, floral displays and fruit arrangements — matching flower type, colour and size to the occasion and the budget. My work has run through high-traffic retail, including the floral counter at Carrefour Yas Mall in Abu Dhabi, and now at Ambrosia, Fresh Market in Dubai.

I also hold a Diploma in Air-Conditioning and Refrigeration, which is less unusual than it sounds in this trade: it means I understand cold-room and flower-chiller conditions, so stock stays fresh longer and less of it is thrown away.

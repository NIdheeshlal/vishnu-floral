01 Profile

Flowers above, carved fruit below — built as one piece.

I am a florist and floral designer based in Dubai with five years of hands-on U.A.E. experience across high-traffic retail counters and private event work. My day-to-day covers bespoke bouquets, vase and box arrangements, fruit gift baskets, and event decoration — matching flower type, colour and scale to the occasion and to the customer's budget.

What sets my work apart is the signature centrepiece: a full floral composition built above a base of fresh fruit, finished with a watermelon I carve by hand into a swan, a dove, a koi pair or a horse. It is a single piece of two crafts, and it is the thing customers remember.

I also hold a Diploma in Air-Conditioning and Refrigeration, which matters more in this trade than it sounds. I understand chiller and cold-room conditions, so stock keeps longer, wastage drops, and displays stay presentable through a full retail day.

05 The signature piece

One build, two crafts

Ten of the pieces in this portfolio are signature centrepieces. They are not a bouquet placed next to a fruit platter — they are built as a single composition, the floral structure rising from a base of fresh fruit, with a watermelon carved by hand as the focal point.

Each carving is cut to the occasion. A swan or a pair of doves for a wedding. A koi pair for a milestone. A horse, a phoenix, a bird, a cluster of watermelon roses. The flowers are chosen around the carving, not the other way round.

It is the part of the job that takes the longest and the part nobody forgets.
